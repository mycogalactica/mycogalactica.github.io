For investors

MycoGalactica offers a strategic entry point into the rapidly expanding lunar economy, projected to grow exponentially as initiatives like NASA’s Artemis program establish a permanent human presence on the Moon.

Our core innovation, MycoBlocks, solves the single greatest barrier to deep-space habitation: the prohibitive cost of transporting radiation shielding from Earth. By leveraging In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) through microwave sintering of lunar regolith and the bio-regeneration of radiotrophic Cladosporium sphaerospermum, we provide a scalable, modular construction solution that offers up to 80% radiation reduction.

This technology doesn't just lower mission costs; it enables the very feasibility of long-term lunar settlement, positioning MycoGalactica as an essential infrastructure partner for space agencies like NASA and ESA, as well as private titans such as SpaceX and Blue Origin.
